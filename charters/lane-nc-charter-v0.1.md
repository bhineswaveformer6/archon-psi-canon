## I · Purpose

Lane NC houses the thesis and measurement-stack canon for **brain-capital economics** as applied to employees and high-performance professionals. It is the *theory and pricing* lane — distinct from Lane N, which is the *evidence and biosignal* lane.

## II · Subject

HPBI (Human Performance Brain Index) and Neural Capital Lens — the operating thesis that brain-capital is a measurable, investable, transferable asset class, and that the legacy "cost-center employee" frame is obsolete. The lane covers:

- Definition of brain capital and HPBI as an economic asset
- Measurement stack: instruments, scoring rules, claim taxonomy
- Market framing: comparisons with traditional VC, talent markets, performance markets
- Audit and provenance rules for HPBI claims
- Distribution surfaces (Substack, LinkedIn, etc.) when explicitly listed

## III · Outputs in Scope

1. **Whitepaper canon** — formal long-form articulations of the thesis
2. **Metric definitions** — precise specifications for each HPBI component metric
3. **Claim taxonomy** — categorization rules for what counts as a valid HPBI claim
4. **Market framing** — competitive positioning vs. traditional VC, talent markets, biotech, etc.
5. **Audit rules** — verification protocol for HPBI claims (anchors in Lane N evidence)

## IV · Exclusions (Hard Boundaries)

- **Raw neuro figures, biosignals, EEG/MRI/BCI evidence** → Lane N
- **Brand marks, portraits, aesthetic library** → Lane B
- **Voice register substrate, mythic doctrine, lexicon sources** → Lane V
- **Legal pack, license templates, contracts** → Lane R (not yet opened)
- **Product code, UI components, app builds** → Lane A

When a Lane NC document *references* a Lane N figure, the figure stays in Lane N and Lane NC carries only the citation hash. No duplication.

## V · Lifecycle Rule

Lane NC v0.1 is a **DRAFT** ingestion manifest. Assets enter at DRAFT state with hash + provenance only. Promotion to PROBATION requires:

1. Architect ratification of the asset's role in the canon (Whitepaper / Metric Def / etc.)
2. Mapping to the audit-rule framework
3. No conflict with Lane N evidence or Lane V doctrine

Promotion to SEALED requires:

1. PROBATION clean for ≥7 days
2. No new contradictory substrate ingested
3. Architect explicit seal

## VI · Cross-Lane Anchoring

Every HPBI claim that asserts a measurable phenomenon must anchor to:

- A **Lane N evidence asset** (biosignal, figure, study) if it depends on neural data
- A **canon-infra attestation receipt** if it makes a market or pricing claim

Unanchored claims sit at DRAFT indefinitely.
