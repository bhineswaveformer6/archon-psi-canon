## What ScoringHub Is
The metric switchboard. 24 canonical metrics organized into 3 weighted stacks with thresholds and directionality. ScoringHub feeds L7 — it does not replace it.

## Stack 1 — Cognitive Score (40% of L7)
Stone avg · QTAC² avg · Ω-Gap · Confidence avg · Seal rate · HW verification rate · BS rate · NC/session

## Stack 2 — Governance (35% of L7)
Self-refusal rate · Policy gate pass · Quality avg · Security avg · Eval pass rate · Incidents · PROCEED rate · HOLD rate

## Stack 3 — Field Coherence (25% of L7)
μ-Purity · NCI avg · φ estimate · Gamma spikes · Illusion flags · IMCM health · Coherence avg · Field Ω-Gap

## Key Rule
Each metric has: direction (higher=better or lower=better), LOW/WATCH/GOOD thresholds, source entity, and a description. L7 reads band scores, not raw values.
