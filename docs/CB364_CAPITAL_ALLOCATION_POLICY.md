**Doctrine:** "Maintenance capital only. Governance and field gates still active."

**Allowed:** Full VOLT issuance including BS 3× mints, new builds, new Canon seals, GTM campaigns, Masters Registry additions  
**Required even at GROWTH:** Every irreversible commit needs a DecisionNode record; every Canon seal must pass Three-Tier Honesty Test; self-refusal rate must stay > 10%
