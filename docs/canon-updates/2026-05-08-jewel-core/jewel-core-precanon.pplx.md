# Jewel Core Pre-Canon: Castle Build Infrastructure Upgrade

## Executive summary

The canonical business spine is not a loose collection of apps. It is a castle build: **Jewel Core** as the symbolic-foundational layer, **ARCHON Ψ** as the measurement and canonization instrument, **CortexChain / Neural Capital OS** as the infrastructure layer, and **QuillCrown / Crown Pass** as the narrative-governance surface. This document hardens that system into a pre-canon operating brief for May 8, 2026.

The current positioning should be: **CortexChain is measurement infrastructure for human cognitive output; ARCHON Ψ is the scoring, provenance, and canon ledger instrument; Factorizer, Reality Lens, Constellation OS, CapitalFlowMatrix, and QuillCrown are product surfaces that turn cognitive artifacts into evaluated, governed, and capital-legible assets.**

## Stone canon primitive

**Stone canon statement:** the company is building the foundational infrastructure for cognitive capital: capture human/agent output, score it through QTAC₇ / Stone Score / Ω-Gap, bind it to provenance, and route high-quality outputs into canonical ledgers, capital workflows, and marketplace surfaces.

**Jewel Core role:** Jewel Core is the deep symbolic kernel. It does not replace the product architecture. It upgrades the architecture by giving the castle a mythic, mnemonic, and governance grammar: crown, stone, canon, volt, ledger, gate, and jewel.

**Castle build role:** the castle is the system boundary. It is the defensible operating structure that prevents CortexChain from being perceived as a scattered portfolio. Each build becomes either a wall, tower, gate, archive, market, or instrument.

## Audit finding

The build has strong asset density but weak surface discipline. The main risk is not lack of originality. The main risk is that investors, partners, and collaborators see too many names before they understand the primitive.

### Strengths

- **Defensible primitive:** cognitive-output measurement is a clear infrastructure thesis with room for product, data, governance, and marketplace expansion.
- **Asset graph already exists:** Notion, GitHub, Google Docs, Drive, and prior Perplexity artifacts already contain enough evidence to support a canonical update.
- **Product modularity:** Factorizer, Reality Lens, Constellation OS, CapitalFlowMatrix, QuillCrown, and ARCHON Ψ can be presented as modules rather than separate companies.
- **Narrative depth:** Jewel Core and castle grammar create memorability and internal operating discipline.

### Gaps

- **Name overload:** ARCHON Ψ, CortexChain, Neural Capital OS, Factorizer, Reality Lens, Constellation OS, CapitalFlowMatrix, QuillCrown, Crown Pass, House of Orange, and Jewel Core require hierarchy.
- **Compliance boundary:** VOLT, capital, marketplace, and cognitive capital language should be separated from any token/security implication until legal review.
- **Valuation evidence:** valuation is currently directional because financials, pilots, revenue, retention, signed LOIs, and verified usage metrics are not yet attached to the canon bundle.
- **CRM operating layer:** HubSpot appears connected for read/search operations, but a write path was not surfaced in the available connector tools; prepare importable records if direct HubSpot creation remains unavailable.

## Investor-facing position

Lead with this:

> CortexChain builds measurement infrastructure for human cognitive output. ARCHON Ψ scores and verifies cognitive artifacts through QTAC₇, Stone Score, Ω-Gap, and provenance ledgers, converting high-signal work into capital-legible records for founders, researchers, operators, and high-agency teams.

Do not lead with this:

> Jewel Core, House of Orange, Tarot/Thoth/1788, VOLT, Crown, and canon language.

Use the symbolic layer internally and selectively in high-context creative/cultural settings. Use the measurement-infrastructure layer for investors, customers, and strategic partners.

### Valuation method

The hybrid valuation uses three lenses:

- **Asset-density lens:** number and coherence of existing artifacts, repos, documents, ledgers, and product surfaces.
- **Readiness lens:** whether the system has a working scoring loop, evidence ledger, buyer workflow, and canonical operating map.
- **Comparable lens:** public AI/software infrastructure comps were used only as a sanity check for how markets value scaled AI/data platforms; early private valuation should be discounted until revenue and usage metrics are attached.
